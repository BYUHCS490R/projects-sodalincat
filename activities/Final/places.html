Angkor Wat

Angkor Wat is one of the most famous temples in the world and a symbol of Cambodia. It is known for its ancient stone carvings and sunrise views.

Koh Rong

Koh Rong is an island in the Sihanoukville Province of Cambodia. It’s known for its sandy coves and coral reefs, like those around Koh Rong Pier.

Other Places to Visit

Visitors also enjoy places such as Kampot, coastal beaches, and Mondulkiri province,the Cambodia’s largest and wildest province.
